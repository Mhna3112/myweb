Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the application "mhna3112" (the "App") handles information. The App is a personal tool that its owner uses to schedule and publish videos to their own TikTok account.

1. Information we access

  • TikTok account information: when you connect a TikTok account, the App receives your open ID, display name and avatar (scope user.info.basic).
  • Publishing permissions: the App uses the video.publish and video.upload scopes only to publish or upload videos that you select.
  • Videos and captions: video files and captions you choose are stored on the device where the App runs.

2. How we use information

We use this information only to: show the connected account, upload and publish the videos you choose, check publishing status, and generate caption suggestions when you request them.

3. Storage

The App runs on the owner's own computer. Access tokens, post data and uploaded videos are stored locally on that computer. We do not operate a central server that collects your data.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. Data is shared only with:

  • TikTok, to authorize your account and publish the videos you choose;
  • Anthropic, when you use the caption feature: the topic, description and tone you enter are sent to the Claude API to generate captions. Do not enter sensitive personal information there.

5. Retention and deletion

You can disconnect an account in the App at any time, which removes its stored tokens. You can delete posts and their video files in the App. You can also revoke the App's access in your TikTok settings.

6. Your choices

Connecting a TikTok account is optional and can be reversed at any time. You control which videos are published and with which privacy settings.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the most recent revision.
